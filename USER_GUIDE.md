# Kiedas Orbiter - In-Depth User Guide

Welcome to the comprehensive guide for Kiedas Orbiter! This document explains how to make the most out of every feature, tab, and setting in the app.

---

## 1. Dashboard & Status

The Dashboard is your command center. It gives you an at-a-glance view of your current state in Warframe and upcoming events.

- **Status Bar:** The top bar indicates if Kiedas Orbiter is successfully reading Warframe session data.
- **World Cycles:** Track the time of day in Cetus, Orb Vallis, Cambion Drift, and Zariman to plan your open-world runs.
- **Active Alerts:** Fissures, Invasions, Sorties, and Archon Hunts are displayed here in real time.

---

## 2. Market Hub & 1-Click Warframe.Market Trading

The **Market** hub consolidates your active Warframe.Market orders and tradeable inventory into a unified trading workstation.

### Active Orders
- **Real-Time Synchronization:** View all your active Sell and Buy orders on Warframe.Market.
- **Order Management:** Update prices or delete listings with a single click without opening a browser.

### Tradeable Stock & Decision Engine
- **Inventory Valuation:** Automatically surfaces all tradable items you currently own with live Warframe.Market pricing and Ducat values.
- **Smart Decision Tags:**
  - `Sell for Plat`: High platinum value compared to ducat worth—best sold on the market.
  - `Ducat Trash`: Low platinum value relative to ducat yield—ideal for Baro Ki'Teer trades.
  - `Keep to Build`: Blueprints or parts needed to complete unmastered weapons or Warframes in your Foundry.
- **1-Click WFM Listing:** Click **Sell on WFM** directly from any stock card to list the item on Warframe.Market. You can customize the price or quantity before listing.
- **Dark Custom Sorting Menu:** Sort by Platinum, Ducats, Ratio, or Name with seamless search retention.

---

## 3. Relic Planner & In-Game Overlay

The Relic Planner takes the guesswork out of cracking relics, finding missing parts, and maximizing Ducat or Platinum returns.

### Relic Planner
- **Missing & Never Obtained Filters:** Instantly filters Prime parts down to items you have never mastered or do not currently hold enough of to build.
- **Add All Missing:** One-click queue planning to target all missing parts across your uncompleted catalog.
- **Owned Relics & Refinement Tallies:** Accurately counts and groups all refinement tiers (Intact, Exceptional, Flawless, Radiant) for every relic in your collection.
- **Instant Response:** Powered by an optimized constant-time Map indexing engine for instant tab switching and filtering.

### In-Game Overlay (OCR)
1. When you are at the **Fissure Reward Selection Screen** in-game, press the assigned hotkey (default is `Ctrl+Shift+R`, customizable in Settings).
2. The app reads the rewards on screen using OCR and cross-references live market data and your inventory.
3. An overlay appears showing the Platinum value, Ducat value, vault status, and mastery badges for each choice.

---

## 4. Riven Market & Neural Valuation

Evaluating Riven mods is automated with built-in neural pricing models.

- **Screenshot Analysis:** Press your Riven OCR hotkey while hovering over a Riven mod in your inventory.
- **Stat Parsing:** Reads positive attributes, negative curses, and weapon compatibility.
- **Offline Valuation:** Uses local ONNX neural network rankings to give you an estimated market valuation.

---

## 5. Foundry, Inventory & Collectibles

- **Live Inventory Sync:** Syncs live inventory with your account, tracking crafted counts, component blueprints, and mastery.
- **Landing Crafts Tracking:** Dedicated tracking for all 7 landing crafts (Liset, Mantis, Scimitar, Xiphos, Nightwave, Skaut, Parallax) with full component breakdown.
- **Foundry Ingredients:** See exactly what resources, components, and prerequisite items are needed before starting a build.

---

## 6. Settings & Support

- **Warframe.Market Token:** Enter your Warframe.Market API Token in Settings to enable direct 1-Click trading.
- **Safe Mode Logging:** Read from Warframe's `EE.log` for background event tracking.
- **OCR Hotkeys & Scaling:** Configure custom screen capture hotkeys and UI scaling factors for 1080p, 1440p, or 4K displays.
- **Issue Reporting:** Click "Report Issue" to package relevant logs and open a GitHub issue.
