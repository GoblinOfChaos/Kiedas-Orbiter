## A. #39 false Market Blueprint price
ExportRecipes `creditsCost` is copied to `blueprintCost` in src/lib/acquisitionInfo.js (~line 249) and shown as "Blueprint: N Credits" and "Market Blueprint: N Credits" (resolveBlueprintOrigin in acquisitionData.js ~14945), but recipes with `excludeFromMarket: true` (e.g. AshPrimeBlueprint: creditsCost 25000, excludeFromMarket true) are NOT sold in the Market. Fix: only treat creditsCost as a Market blueprint price when the recipe is not excludeFromMarket (carry an explicit flag through the recipe index). Where the cost chip "Blueprint: N" is not a real market price, do not show it. Before relying on the meaning of the field, check a few non-Prime items that DO have creditsCost and are not excluded, and report what you found (wiki.warframe.com only if you need to verify; no Fandom).

## B. #36 Blueprint row for Prime items
`resolveBlueprintOrigin` returns null for Prime items whose blueprint drops from relics (92 of 166 Prime drawers have no "1x Blueprint" row). Fix: for Prime items always show the "1x <Item> Blueprint" requirement row in BOTH drawers (PreviewAcquisitionDrawer.jsx and AcquisitionDrawer.jsx), listing the blueprint's relics one row per relic with its Common/Uncommon/Rare tier (use buildIngredientSourceRows / getRelicSourceLabel from src/lib/relicSources.js and the blueprint component's drops), falling back to the existing origin text for non-relic blueprints. ## C. #9 reopened: Warframe drawer relic rows
On the Ash Prime Warframe drawer, "1x Ash Prime Neuroptics" still lists "Axi N2 Relic (Radiant) - Ash Prime Neuroptics Blueprint 20.0%", then "(Flawless) 17.0%". Cause (partly verified): the fallback drops come from bundled item data as plain rows (location like "Axi N2 Relic" or "Axi B1 Relic (Radiant)", chance 16.67) that are NOT typed `relic`, so normalizeRelicSources skips them. Fix: treat any drop whose location/name is a relic (with or without a refinement suffix) as a relic source, collapse to one row per relic, tier from ExportRelics rarity (the way Citrine Prime Blueprint already works), no refinement, no percentage. Apply to Prime Warframe, weapon and companion drawers. Real-data test via the same drawer data path on Ash Prime, Citrine Prime and one Prime weapon: no "(Radiant|Flawless|Exceptional|Intact)", no "%", each relic once. NOTE the probe harness may resolve names in another language; confirm the harness language and use English names.
