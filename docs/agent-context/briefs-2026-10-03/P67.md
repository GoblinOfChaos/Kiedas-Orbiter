## Rule (decided by the owner)
- A weapon is Incarnon = yes when its own `EvolutionProgress` record exists in the raw inventory (an installed or innate Incarnon on that weapon). Only that weapon is marked.
- A weapon is also Incarnon = yes when the owner holds the Incarnon adapter for its family (an `...IncarnonUnlocker` item in the raw inventory's MiscItems), and the weapon is in that family. Every owned weapon in that family is marked, because one adapter fits every variant.
