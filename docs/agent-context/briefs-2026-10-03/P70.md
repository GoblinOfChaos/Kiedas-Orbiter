## Facts from the audit (`docs/agent-context/reports/filter-audit.md`, check 1)
The Primary, Secondary and Melee buttons on the Inventory Weapons tab match only `item.weapon_type` values. 171 weapons match none: 128 other equipment (Archwings, Amps, Grimoire and similar), 37 Zaws and 6 Kitguns. Zaws and Kitguns have no `weapon_type`. Their buckets are `zaws` and `kitguns` in the parsed inventory.

## Decision (owner)
Add a Zaw button and a Kitgun button next to Primary, Secondary and Melee. Keep the 128 other equipment out of the type buttons. Nothing may be silently dropped: those 128 stay visible under All, and must not match Primary, Secondary, Melee, Zaw or Kitgun.

## Steps
1. Read `FILTER_GROUPS` and the type-button definitions in `src/screens/Inventory.jsx` (around lines 150-200 and the predicates around lines 656-703). Read how `weapon_type` is set in `src/lib/inventoryParser.js` (around lines 1784-1797).
2. Add the two buttons. Their predicate must match exactly the zaws and kitguns buckets, using a stable marker the parser already sets (for example `item.category === 'zaws'` / `'kitguns'`). Do not change `weapon_type` for existing items unless the diff is one line and the audit shows no other effect.
3. Do not change the Primary, Secondary or Melee predicates.
4. Labels: reuse existing translation keys if the app already has keys for "Zaw" and "Kitgun"; otherwise add the key to all 15 locale files under `src/lib/i18n/` in the same change, and say which keys were added. Do not invent English text without a key in every locale.
