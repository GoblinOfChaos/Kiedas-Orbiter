## Rule (same as the relic and item rules already merged)
For a Prime Set, look at each relic that drops one of its parts:
- Vaulted: every relic source of the set's parts is a confirmed-vaulted relic (vault date in the past, or a Baro relic is never vaulted).
- Unknown: at least one relic source is unknown (no vault date), and none is confirmed available.
- Available: at least one relic source is confirmed available (vault date in the future, or no vaulted state and not unknown).
- A set with no relic sources at all is unknown, not vaulted.
