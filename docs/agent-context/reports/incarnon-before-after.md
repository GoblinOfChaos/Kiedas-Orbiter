121 weapon flag states changed: 77 became no and 44 became yes.

## Weapons that became no

All 77 lost the flag because they have neither their own `EvolutionProgress` record nor membership in an owned exact adapter family. First eight only: Afentis Prime, Alternox Prime, Ax-52, Boar Prime, Bubonico, Burston Prime, Cedo Prime, and Coda Bassocyst.

## Weapons that became yes

All 44 gained the flag through an owned exact adapter family. First eight only: Boltor and Boltor Prime (Boltor adapter); Braton and Braton Vandal (Braton adapter); Dera and Dera Vandal (Dera adapter); Gorgon and Gorgon Wraith (Gorgon adapter).
