### `/Lotus/Powersuits/Choir/Choir` (Jade)
- **Existing:** Main Blueprint acquired upon completing the Jade Shadows quest. Component blueprints drop from Ascension on Brutus, Uranus (5% chance each), or can be purchased from Ordis in the Drifter's Camp (or Larunda Relay, Mercury during Operation: Belly of the Beast) using Vestigial Motes - 150 per component, 450 for the main blueprint (900 total).

### `/Lotus/Types/Friendly/Pets/CreaturePets/ArmoredInfestedCatbrowPetPowerSuit` (Panzer Vulpaphyla)
- **Existing:** Captured as a Weakened Panzer Vulpaphyla in the Cambion Drift via Conservation mechanics (attacked into a weakened state by roaming Infested, then tranquilized), then revivified by Son at the Necralisk for 5,000 Credits along with a chosen Mutagen and Antigen.
- **New:** Vulpaphylas are acquired through Revivification with Son in the Necralisk, Deimos. The player must capture a Weakened Panzer Vulpaphyla via Conservation (found only during Fass cycle) after it has been attacked by Infested, as well as obtain a Mutagen and Antigen bought from Son with Entrati Standing, and 5,000 to initiate the Revivification process.

A newly revived Vulpaphyla cannot be renamed, customized, Polarized or earn Mastery Rank experience, but can equip an Orokin Reactor. In order to unlock these features, the player must Gild their Vulpaphyla by achieving rank of Acquaintance with the Entrati, leveling the Vulpaphyla up to Rank 30 and then returning to Son along with 10 Son Token and 5,000.
- **Source:** article page: Panzer Vulpaphyla (##Acquisition section)
