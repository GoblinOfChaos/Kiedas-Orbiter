# Structured vendor acquisition supplement

The Nightwave entries added to `wiki-vendors-acquisition.json` are supported by
the Warframe Wiki's Nightwave acquisition material. The page identifies the
listed augment and weapon names as Nightwave rewards or Nightwave Offerings;
the app intentionally renders the stable route `Sold by Nightwave.` instead of
copying historical series/rank text that can become stale.

Source: <https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nightwave/Offerings>

The exact export/WFCD unique name remains the app's identity key; this
supplement only supplies the acquisition route.
