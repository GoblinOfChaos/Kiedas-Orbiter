**Amalgam Daikyu Target Acquired** is an Amalgam mod exclusive to the [](/w/Daikyu "Daikyu") [Daikyu](/w/Daikyu "Daikyu") and [](/w/Daikyu_Prime "Daikyu Prime") [Daikyu Prime](/w/Daikyu_Prime "Daikyu Prime"). It provides an increased [headshot](/w/Enemy_Body_Parts "Enemy Body Parts") multiplier, [Life Steal](/w/Life_Steal "Life Steal") when using [Nikanas](/w/Nikanas "Nikanas"), and a chance to gather used arrows.

## Notes

  * With this mod equipped, arrows stuck in enemies or on surfaces can sometimes be picked up when physically touched, along with a unique sound upon pickup. Multiple arrows fired from [multishot](/w/Multishot "Multishot") may also be picked up, occasionally refunding more ammo than what was expended.
  * Since headshots normally reward 3.0x damage, this mod will increase that to 5.25x damage. This is further increased with [](/w/Primary_Deadhead "Primary Deadhead") [Primary Deadhead](/w/Primary_Deadhead "Primary Deadhead") for a total headshot damage multiplier of 6.15x.
  * Not [tradeable](/w/Trading "Trading").
  * The life steal granted to [Nikanas](/w/Nikana_\(Weapon_Type\) "Nikana \(Weapon Type\)") applies to all of their attacks, including the projectile heavy attack of [](/w/Syam "Syam") [Syam](/w/Syam "Syam"). 
    * Damage ticks from status effects do not result in life steal.
    * Lifesteal does not apply to [Two-Handed Nikanas](/w/Two-Handed_Nikana "Two-Handed Nikana"), [Dual Nikanas](/w/Dual_Nikanas "Dual Nikanas"), or [Exalted Blade](/w/Exalted_Blade "Exalted Blade").
    * Lifesteal does not interact with [](/w/Archon_Intensify "Archon Intensify") [Archon Intensify](/w/Archon_Intensify "Archon Intensify").

## Tips

  * The mod can synergize very well with [](/w/Ivara "Ivara") [Ivara](/w/Ivara "Ivara")'s [](/w/Prowl "Prowl") [Prowl](/w/Prowl "Prowl") ability as that also has innate bonus damage to headshots, and the Daikyu is innately silent.
