A classic Orokin weapon, Braton Prime features modified damage levels and a larger magazine over the standard model.

The Braton Prime is the Prime variant of the  Braton, featuring higher damage, critical chance, critical multiplier, status chance, fire rate, magazine size, and ammo reserves, at the cost of reload speed.

Incarnon Genesis

This section is transcluded from Braton Incarnon Genesis § Acquisition. To change it, please edit the transcluded page.
  • Upon completion of The Duviri Paradox and Angels of the Zariman, and unlocking The Steel Path, players can earn  Braton Incarnon Genesis from The Circuit's Steel Path to place on their Braton with the help of Cavalero in the Chrysalith. This Incarnon Genesis form grants it evolutions to a transformation and newfound strength.
  • Purchased from Cavalero for 120 Platinum 120, complete with the resources used in its installation. This purchase can only be made once.
This section is transcluded from Braton Incarnon Genesis § Incarnon Genesis. To change it, please edit the transcluded page.
  • Installing the Braton Incarnon Genesis requires 20  Pathos Clamps, 60  Rune Marrow, and 60  Tasoma Extract.
    • When installed, the first Evolution tier is unlocked. Each evolution challenge additionally grants 5,000 affinity to the weapon, affected by Affinity Boosters.

Overview

  • Incarnon Form is charged by filling the Incarnon Transmutation gauge that appears under the reticle by achieving weakpoint hits and then pressing the Alternate Fire button. 20 weakpoints will completely fill the gauge. A full gauge is not required to activate Incarnon Form.
    • Charges do generate when hitting weak points highlighted by  Sonar or  Detect Vulnerability.
      • However, they do not generate from hits on  Crystallize's crystalline growths.
    • Weakpoints on corpses or Invulnerable targets do not build charges.
    • Individual Multishot bullets can build charges.
  • Mode switching is not instantaneous and has an animation equal to the weapon's reload speed.
    • Swapping to and from Incarnon Form will fully reload the normal form's magazine.
    • Swapping to normal mode while there are charges remaining will waste those charges.
  • Instead of drawing ammunition from its reserves, the Braton's Incarnon Form uses a separate "magazine" with each charge producing 10 rounds to a maximum of 200. Once all ammunition is expended, the Braton reverts to its normal form.
    • Incarnon Form's magazine is not affected by mods.
    • Incarnon Form is not affected by Ammo Efficiency (such as  Energized Munitions).
    • Charges and Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, bleedout, or being disarmed. However, charges and Incarnon Form are removed upon death.

Tips

  • When fighting at long range, consider firing the Braton Prime in short bursts, or charge the enemy to gain ground. The ammo saved from avoiding long range fighting can compensate for damage sustained in melee/short ranged combat.
  • A higher pitch firing sound can be heard when the current magazine is nearly out of ammo.
