A classic Orokin weapon, Braton Prime features modified damage levels and a larger magazine over the standard model.

### Overview

  * Incarnon Form is charged by filling the Incarnon Transmutation gauge that appears under the reticle by achieving [weakpoint hits](/w/Enemy_Body_Parts "Enemy Body Parts") and then pressing the [Alternate Fire](/w/Alternate_Fire "Alternate Fire") button. **20** weakpoints will completely fill the gauge. A full gauge is not required to activate Incarnon Form. 
    * Charges **do** generate when hitting weak points highlighted by [](/w/Sonar "Sonar") [Sonar](/w/Sonar "Sonar") or [](/w/Detect_Vulnerability "Detect Vulnerability") [Detect Vulnerability](/w/Detect_Vulnerability "Detect Vulnerability"). 
      * However, they do not generate from hits on [](/w/Crystallize "Crystallize") [Crystallize](/w/Crystallize "Crystallize")'s crystalline growths.
    * Weakpoints on corpses or [Invulnerable](/w/Invulnerable "Invulnerable") targets do **not** build charges.
    * Individual [Multishot](/w/Multishot "Multishot") bullets can build charges.
  * Mode switching is **not** instantaneous and has an animation equal to the weapon's [reload speed](/w/Reload_speed "Reload speed"). 
    * Swapping to and from Incarnon Form will fully reload the normal form's magazine.
    * Swapping to normal mode while there are charges remaining will waste those charges.
  * Instead of drawing ammunition from its reserves, the Braton's Incarnon Form uses a separate "magazine" with each charge producing **10** rounds to a maximum of **200**. Once all ammunition is expended, the Braton reverts to its normal form. 
    * Incarnon Form's magazine is **not** affected by mods.
    * Incarnon Form is **not** affected by [Ammo Efficiency](/w/Ammo_Efficiency "Ammo Efficiency") (such as [](/w/Energized_Munitions "Energized Munitions") [Energized Munitions](/w/Energized_Munitions "Energized Munitions")).
    * Charges and Incarnon Form persists through all activities, such as holstering, [bleedout](/w/Bleedout "Bleedout"), or being [disarmed](/w/Disarm "Disarm"). However, charges and Incarnon Form are removed upon [death](/w/Death "Death").

## Tips

  * When fighting at long range, consider firing the Braton Prime in short bursts, or charge the enemy to gain ground. The ammo saved from avoiding long range fighting can compensate for damage sustained in melee/short ranged combat.
  * A higher pitch firing sound can be heard when the current magazine is nearly out of ammo.
