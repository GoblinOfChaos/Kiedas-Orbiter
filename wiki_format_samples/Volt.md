Electricity flows through Volt. His attacks deal high damage. Enemies will be shocked.

> This is Volt, a versatile warrior. Volt's arsenal is powerful and electrifying. Handle with care, Tenno.—[Lotus](/w/Lotus "Lotus")

**Release Date:** October 25th, 2012 

Electrical currents surge wildly through his veins, generating lethal sparks and chain lightning in his wake. Ready to race against time with his fellow [](/w/Tenno "Tenno") [Tenno](/w/Tenno "Tenno"), short-circuit robotics and overload organics as the Live Wire, **Volt**. Bend his electricity into an impenetrable barrier, or convert the shocked masses into deadly Tesla coils. Choose wisely, Tenno. Start your new journey with Volt as one of three beginner Warframes. 

**[](/w/Volt "Volt") Volt** is a [Warframe](/w/Warframe "Warframe") with [](/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage "Damage/Electricity Damage") [Electricity](/w/Damage/Electricity_Damage "Damage/Electricity Damage") powers to deal damage. His [Passive](/w/Passive "Passive") builds electricity and discharges it in his next attack. [](/w/Shock "Shock") [Shock](/w/Shock "Shock") releases an electric projectile. [](/w/Speed "Speed") [Speed](/w/Speed "Speed") energizes his and his allies with enhanced [Movement Speed](/w/Movement_Speed "Movement Speed"), [Attack Speed](/w/Attack_Speed "Attack Speed"), and [Reload Speed](/w/Reload_Speed "Reload Speed"). [](/w/Electric_Shield "Electric Shield") [Electric Shield](/w/Electric_Shield "Electric Shield") projects a barrier that blocks incoming fire while strengthening allied gunfire that passes through it and can be carried around. [](/w/Discharge "Discharge") [Discharge](/w/Discharge "Discharge") releases a powerful pulse that emits arcs of electricity on afflicted enemies. 

## Acquisition

Volt is one of the three starter Warframes, alongside [](/w/Excalibur "Excalibur") [Excalibur](/w/Excalibur "Excalibur") and [](/w/Mag "Mag") [Mag](/w/Mag "Mag"), during [Awakening](/w/Awakening "Awakening"). 

Volt's blueprints can be [researched](/w/Research "Research") from the [Tenno Lab](/w/Research/Tenno_Lab "Research/Tenno Lab") in the [dojo](/w/Dojo "Dojo"). 

Volt can also be bought complete from [Teshin](/w/Teshin "Teshin") for **60,000 [Standing](/w/Standing "Standing")[](/w/Standing "Standing") 60,000** once players have achieved **Typhoon** rank in the [Conclave](/w/Conclave "Conclave"). 

### Historical

  * Prior to [Update 12.0](/w/Update_12#Update_12.0 "Update 12") (2014-02-05), Volt's component blueprints dropped from [Survival](/w/Survival "Survival") missions.
  * Prior to [Update 11.0](/w/Update_11#Update_11.0 "Update 11") (2013-11-20), Volt's component blueprints dropped from [J3-Golem](/w/J3-Golem "J3-Golem") on [Themisto](/w/Themisto "Themisto"), [Jupiter](/w/Jupiter "Jupiter").[1]

## Trivia

  * The volt is a measure of electrical potential, a type of potential energy that could be released if an electrical current is allowed to flow, hence Volt's electrical essence.
  * During closed beta, Overload was notoriously overpowered as a result of a bug that would allow the lightning to daisy-chain throughout a level, killing any enemy that had spawned.
  * Before [Update 7](/w/Update_7 "Update 7") Volt was one of the 3 initial starter Frames. He was replaced by [](/w/Mag "Mag") [Mag](/w/Mag "Mag") due to a desire of the developers to have a female starter. Volt then replaced [](/w/Loki "Loki") [Loki](/w/Loki "Loki") as a starting Warframe in [Update 14](/w/Update_14 "Update 14"), becoming the only Warframe to be a starting option during two separate periods.
  * Volt has had three drop changes since [Update 11](/w/Update_11 "Update 11"); prior to Update 11 his components dropped from the [J3-Golem](/w/J3-Golem "J3-Golem") on Jupiter. After [The Hunt For Alad V](/w/The_Hunt_For_Alad_V "The Hunt For Alad V"), his components were moved to [Survival](/w/Survival "Survival") mission rewards, making him the second Warframe obtainable through regular mission rewards (the first was [](/w/Banshee "Banshee") [Banshee](/w/Banshee "Banshee")). [Update 12](/w/Update_12 "Update 12") moved Volt and Banshee to the Tenno Research Labs, and the both of them have remained there since.
  * The rings on Volt's arms are considered customizable [armor](/w/Warframe_Cosmetics#Armor "Warframe Cosmetics") parts and will be removed if any other arm plates are used.
  * Volt is the first Warframe to receive 5 Augment Mods, 2 of them being [Conclave](/w/Conclave "Conclave") exclusive.
  * Volt's Neuroptics is a requisite ingredient for [](/w/Chroma "Chroma") [Chroma](/w/Chroma "Chroma").
  * Volt's Systems is a requisite ingredient for [Shocking Step Ephemera](/w/Shocking_Step_Ephemera "Shocking Step Ephemera").
